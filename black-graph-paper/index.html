What black graph paper is for

Write on it with a white gel pen, a metallic or silver marker, or a white pencil. Regular graphite barely shows on black.

Dark design work

Wireframe and UI mock-ups read cleanly in white-on-black, and a dark sheet flatters screenshots and pitch decks.

Light art & photography

White lines on black photograph well and make a strong backdrop for pixel-art and light-painting layouts.

Low-glare, high contrast

The white grid holds ~95% contrast against the page, so spacing checks stay easy even from arm’s length.

  • White grid lines, near-black background (#12171B).
  • High contrast for printer-friendly line work.
  • Slightly more ink to print than a white sheet — expect a dark page.

Questions about black graph paper

Does printing the black background waste a lot of ink?

A full black page uses noticeably more toner than a white one — most of the sheet is covered. If ink is a concern, print one sheet first, and set your print dialog to “Draft” or “Eco” mode if your printer offers it. The grid stays perfectly usable.

Can I write on it in pencil after printing?

Yes, though a soft white or light pen reads best. For pencil work people usually prefer the cream version, which is nearly as kind to the eye but costs far less ink.

Is the black version really the same grid as the white one?

Exactly the same geometry — same line spacing and same measuring bars. Only the background changes, so swap freely between the three colour versions without re-learning the grid.

Which square size should I pick?

5 mm (metric) or 1/4 inch (US) is the everyday notebook grid. Go finer, 2 mm or 10 per inch, for detailed plotting; go bigger, 1 cm, 1/2 inch or 1 inch, for young learners, floor plans and anything you want to read from a distance.
