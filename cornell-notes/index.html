Three areas for structured notes

Use the left cue column for questions and keywords, the ruled right section for notes, and the bottom summary area for the main ideas. Adjust the cue width, summary height and line spacing in the generator.

Print your Cornell notes template

Choose A4 or US Letter to match your paper. Download the PDF and print at 100% or Actual Size. The measuring bars help you check the scale; you can turn them off in the extra settings.

Can I customize this template?

Yes. Change the cue column width and summary height in millimetres, select college, wide or narrow ruling, and add a sheet title or name/date header in the extra settings. The preview and PDF use the same geometry.
