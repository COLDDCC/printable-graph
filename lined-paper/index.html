Lined paper for notes, school and everyday writing

Choose college ruled (9/32 inch), wide ruled (11/32 inch), or narrow ruled (1/4 inch), or enter your own line spacing. Download a free PDF on A4, US Letter or a custom paper size. Add an optional red left margin line, change the line color, and print as many copies as you need.

Print at the correct size

Match the selected paper size to your printer and choose 100% or Actual Size. Disable Fit to page. Keep the measuring bars enabled to check the printed spacing with a ruler.

Which ruling should I choose?

College ruled has approximately 7.14 mm spacing for everyday notes. Wide ruled provides approximately 8.73 mm for larger handwriting. Narrow ruled uses 6.35 mm to fit more writing on each page.
